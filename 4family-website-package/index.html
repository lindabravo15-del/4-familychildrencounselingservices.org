What We Offer

Counseling built around behavior, family, and childhood development

Family Therapy

Improving communication and strengthening family bonds. Trauma-informed family counseling for lasting healing and growth throughout Riverside and San Bernardino County.

Child Therapy

Play-based, age-appropriate therapy that helps young children process big emotions, anxiety, transitions, and behavioral challenges in a safe, welcoming space.

Teen Counseling

A judgment-free space for adolescents working through identity, peer pressure, school stress, self-esteem, and family conflict.

Behavioral Support

Structured, evidence-based behavior intervention for children and teens navigating ADHD, defiance, emotional regulation, and social skill-building.

Parent Coaching

Practical tools and one-on-one coaching that help parents respond with confidence to tantrums, boundaries, discipline, and everyday parenting stress.

Trauma-Informed Care

A gentle, trauma-informed approach for children, teens, and families healing from grief, loss, divorce, or difficult life transitions.
